How it works

3

4

Boundaries and wickets animate by themselves. Everything else is one click in the control room.

What is inside

Built for real tournaments

Ball-by-ball ledger

Append-only. Undo and corrections rebuild every statistic, never silently delete history.

Tournaments

Fixtures, points table, net run rate with all-out rule, public tournament page.

Teams & players

Logos, colours, roles, squads. Player statistics come from recorded deliveries.

Event-driven graphics

FOUR, SIX, WICKET, fifty, hundred, hat-trick, no-ball, maiden. Fired by deliveries, not score digits.

Secure links

High-entropy tokens, stored hashed, revocable, read-only. Scoring and broadcast permissions are separate.

Public scoreboard

Mobile-first live page with scorecard, partnerships and fall of wickets. Share link included.
